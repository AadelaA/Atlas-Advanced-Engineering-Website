Who
are we

Atlas Advanced Engineering is a student‑led engineering organization founded on curiosity, creativity, and practical exploration. We bring together students who are passionate about engineering and project development. We aim to create an engineering community where members can learn, experiment, and build multi‑domain projects that integrate into larger systems and operate together as one.
